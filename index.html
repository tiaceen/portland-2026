A weekend guide · food · coffee · hikes · neighborhoods

Friday

Morning
Good Coffee
Portland's beloved local chain right in Troutdale — grab your morning coffee on the way to the falls. Clean, modern, great lattes.
Mid-Morning
Latourell Falls Hike
Easy 2.4 mile loop, stunning waterfall, minimal elevation gain. About 1–1.5 hours. Drive back ~30–40 min to Portland.
4:00 PM
Kati Portland
4.7 star vegan Thai on SE Division — pad see ew, curries, Thai tots. Open until 8:45pm. Right on the Division Street shopping corridor — pop into shops before or after.

Saturday

8:45 AM
Screen Door
Southern brunch, famous chicken and waffles. Walk-in only, no reservations. Close to the Alberta Arts District — great neighborhood to walk around after.
Evening
Cartopia Food Cart Pod
Portland's iconic food cart pod on SE Hawthorne — Mexican, Thai, Egyptian, pizza, great variety. Right on the Hawthorne Blvd shopping strip.

Sunday

Morning
Portland Lux Coffee
Hidden gem on outer SE Division. Beautiful cozy atmosphere, amazing pastries and desserts. 4.7 stars.
Late Morning

Kachka

☕ Coffee Spots

On the Way to the Falls
Rivertrails Cafe & Espresso
Right in Troutdale on the Historic Columbia River Hwy — solid espresso, great bagel sandwiches. 4.8 ⭐
Good Coffee (Troutdale)
Portland's beloved local chain, right across from Rivertrails. Clean, modern, great lattes. Opens 7am Fridays. 4.4 ⭐
Eastside Portland
Heretic Coffee Co.
Volunteer run, roasts their own beans, makes their own syrups. Gorgeous space on SE 28th Ave. 4.9 ⭐
Portland Lux Coffee
Hidden gem on outer SE Division. Beautiful cozy atmosphere, amazing desserts and pastries. 4.7 ⭐
Cadejo Coffee
Mexican-inspired — mole mocha, café de olla latte, chilaquiles. 4.9 ⭐
Portland Icons
Stumptown Coffee Roasters
Portland's most iconic roaster. Multiple locations around the city — a must at least once.
Deadstock Coffee
Sneaker-themed, "snob-free." Signature drinks named after athletes — Steph Curry (white chocolate mocha), Luther Vandross (lavender mocha). Now at The Hoxton hotel.

Things To Do

Must-Do
Powell's Books
World's largest independent bookstore — an entire city block. Could spend hours here. In the Pearl District; 23rd Ave is a short walk away.
Pittock Mansion
Historic mansion with stunning views of Mt. Hood and the Portland skyline. 4.7 ⭐
International Rose Test Garden
Free, iconic Portland. Thousands of rose varieties, beautiful terraced walkways. 4.8 ⭐
Portland Japanese Garden
Peaceful and stunning, right next to the Rose Garden. Feels like Japan. 4.6 ⭐
Nature
Forest Park
Largest urban forest in the US. Great trails through old-growth forest, very Portland. 4.8 ⭐
Hoyt Arboretum
Free, beautiful tree walks including a stunning redwood grove. 4.9 ⭐
Lan Su Chinese Garden
Traditional Chinese garden tucked in the city with a tea house inside. 4.7 ⭐
Neighborhoods & Shopping
Pearl District
Galleries, design shops, boutiques — upscale but approachable, very walkable. Near Powell's & Nong's Khao Man Gai.
Alberta Arts District
Murals everywhere, local boutiques, vintage shops, very creative neighborhood vibe. Near Screen Door.
Mississippi Avenue
Indie shops, cute cafes — one of Portland's most charming and walkable streets.
Hawthorne Blvd
Vintage clothing, bookshops, eclectic local stores — very Portland. Near Cartopia & Kachka.
Division Street
Lots of cute spots to pop into — right where Kati Portland is.
23rd Avenue (NW)
Nicknamed "Trendy-Third" — boutiques, cafes, restaurants, very walkable and cute. Near Powell's Books.
